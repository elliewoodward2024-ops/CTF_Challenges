Crypt Challenge 7 ×

This was a little bit more obscure, but after reading or trail you could find out that this style with 'a' and 'b' is a Affine cypher. Then covert letters to numbers (A=0, Z=25), find the modular inverse ((a*a^{-1})(mod26) = 1 ), apply decryption formula (P = a^{-1}*(C - b)(mod26)), and lastly covert numbers back to letters. If you don't want to do all that you could use the Cryptii or Boxentriq tool. Then just wrap your answer in ctf{}.

Crypt Challenge 8 ×

Once again more of an obscure hint and cypher. This is a Playfair cypher. To solve this playfair cypher you first want to make the 5*5 grid replacing the first letters with the key words, then filling the rest of the grid with the rest of the alphabet combining I and J to fit 25 letters. You would then want to spilt the cypher text into pairings. Apply the main rules of the cypher. Or you could use the Boxentriq tool.
